# Creative direction for future AI Shorts

This is a human/agent production brief, not an automated prompt: the current renderer uses a fixed script and does not call a language model.

- Write one precise, fact-checked idea for a curious beginner. Open with a concrete question or surprising implication; pay it off with a clear example.
- Use natural spoken English, short sentences and varied rhythm. Avoid hype, unsupported claims and generic calls to follow.
- Plan distinct scenes: action linked to the exact point being spoken, different compositions and restrained, varied colors. Avoid repeating identical cards or filler motion.
- Animate at 30 fps or higher with eased movement, holds and deliberate scene changes. Keep text legible and outside Shorts interface overlays.
- Synthesize complete thoughts, leaving breathing room. Pause longer before the closing question. Avoid claiming that synthesized speech conveys human-level emotion.
- End with a specific question that invites an authentic answer and relates to the topic.
- Check narration, captions, pronunciation, timing, visual quality and factual claims before publishing. Captions in this prototype are approximate and require review.
- Use free open-source or openly licensed tools and assets; check usage licenses. Do not upload to YouTube without explicit user authorization.
